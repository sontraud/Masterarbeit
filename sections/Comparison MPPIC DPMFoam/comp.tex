\chapter{Comparison \MP and \DP}
Two versions of a fluidized bed reactor are pre-implemented in the OpenFOAM Tutorial Folder:
Goldschmidt with the \texttt{DPMFoam} solver and Goldschmidt with the \textttt{MPPICFoam} solver. 
The first solver, which runs for approximately $15$ hours tracks each particle and models their collison. The second solver, which runs for approximately $15$ minutes, interpolates parcel properties to and from an Eulerian grid.
